# DUAL-ASCENT Evidence Register

## Method and inclusion boundary

This register is a targeted, non-exhaustive screen of directly relevant research and sources inspected during the commission. “Full text inspected” means a complete accessible webpage/preprint was read for the stated details. “Abstract inspected” means claims must not exceed the abstract. Evidence confidence concerns applicability to a DUAL-ASCENT protocol, not a judgment of author intent. Peer review, design, independence, and external validity are considered separately.

## Construct map: what can and cannot be measured

| Construct | Defensible observable indicator | Required caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Cognitive offloading | Delegation choice, task demand, aid reliability, later unaided check | Offloading can be rational and beneficial. |
| Automation bias / overreliance | Acceptance of known-wrong advice; missed need to seek information; incorrect rejection of correct advice | Must include both compliance and reliance opportunities; not a trait label. |
| Calibrated trust | Alignment between reliance and measured tool reliability; confidence calibration | Self-reported trust alone is insufficient. |
| Independent transfer | Delayed unaided performance on held-out but structurally related task | A transfer test must not be identical to assisted practice. |
| Agency / autonomy | Authored goals, ability to abstain/revise, goal-artifact traceability, voluntary reflection | Logs cannot infer motive or freedom. |
| Creative diversity | Corpus-level pairwise similarity plus blind human ratings and participant voice report | Diversity is not a universal proxy for value. |
| Tool-first drift | Missing prior goal or declared-exploration contract plus weak later independent value traceability | Do not label curiosity, frequency, or tool discovery as pathology. |
| Prospective prediction | Precommitted next-action prediction compared with independently declared action and baselines | Predictive success is not access to private intentions. |

## Verification and uncertainty log

The cited 2026 “systematic review and meta-analysis” and 2026 *Nature Human Behaviour* linguistic-diversity articles named in the supplied addendum were not independently verified to a standard sufficient for substantive use in this package. They are therefore omitted from the empirical synthesis rather than repeated. The Anthropic-hosted description mentioned by the addendum was cross-checked against the corresponding arXiv preprint; the report cites the preprint and labels its review status and affiliations. No claim in this register depends on unavailable full text for E-007.

## Second-pass source-verification change log

This targeted second pass rechecked the internal classification and citation links in this register against the first-pass source notes and did not add a new substantive empirical source or change the **REVISE** recommendation. E-001 through E-003 retain the first-pass “full text inspected” status; E-004 remains a preprint with an independence caveat; E-007 remains inaccessible beyond publisher/metadata review for this commission; E-009 remains qualitative personal testimony rather than clinical or causal evidence.
