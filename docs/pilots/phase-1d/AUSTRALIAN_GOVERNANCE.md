## Current official framework

As of 2026-11-09, the **NHMRC National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research 2025** is effective. It came into force **23 June 2026** and superseded the 2023 edition. Source: NHMRC official policy page:

https://www.nhmrc.gov.au/research-policy/ethics/national-statement-ethical-conduct-human-research

The NHMRC consultation hub reports a **5 November–16 December 2026** public consultation proposing changes affecting the definition of human research and exemptions. These proposals **are not themselves an ethics determination or presently enacted change**. The research lead must check current rules before the actual application:

https://consultations.nhmrc.gov.au/ethics-integrity/2025-national-statement-update-public-consultation/

The applicable approval, exemption, low-risk review or other route depends on the host institution, method, risks, participants and jurisdiction. **Do not select the path yourself on the basis of this Markdown file.** An independent researcher should contact an appropriate institutional research/ethics office or accredited review body and request an explicit determination. If the proposed study involves different jurisdictions or organisations, record those requirements separately.

## Ethics and data-flow intake checklist for a qualified lead

- Identify the responsible legal/institutional operator and qualified human principal investigator; disclose potential conflicts of interest.
- Define the actual human decision, participant eligibility and recruitment path; account for coercion/dependency risks.
- Describe why the task constitutes human research or not, using current national/institutional guidance; request and **retain** the authoritative written decision.
- Define baseline, allocation, assistance interventions, hidden assessment, delayed follow-up, time burden, foreseen risks and interruption/withdrawal mechanics.
- Document exactly what leaves the participant's device, including use of an external AI provider; freeze provider/version where feasible and review provider retention/data processing.
- Specify consent, data access boundaries, confidential allocation keys, de-identification, retention, destruction, backup handling, breach/incident contact, compensation, complaints and publication.
- Pre-register planned contrasts, scoring, rater agreement threshold, missing-data treatment and stopping/amendment rules before enrollment.
- Retain privately the authorization reference, effective date, custodian and approved protocol version. Publish only a reviewed, non-identifying status summary if appropriate.
